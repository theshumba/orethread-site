A few good questions.

Can we use Orethread today?+

You can explore the interactive demo now. The production platform is in development, and we’re inviting teams to discuss an assisted pilot around a real project.

Does it replace our technical advisers?+

Your team and advisers make the decisions. Orethread is designed to make the supporting evidence easier to find, compare and review.

Do we have to move all our systems?+

The first pilot starts with a selected set of project documents. It is designed to fit around your existing technical tools, with scope agreed before onboarding.

Can we upload confidential files into this demo?+

No upload is needed here. This demonstration uses fictional documents only. Access, storage and data handling for a real pilot will be agreed with your team first.
