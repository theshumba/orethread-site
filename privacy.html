Privacy, plainly.

This is an early-stage website for Orethread, a venture being developed by Melusi Ndoro. This notice was updated on 2 October 2026.

The interactive demo

The project, documents and figures are fictional. The demo does not accept customer documents or send project information to an AI provider. Your demo actions remain in the current page and reset when you reload.

Enquiries

The introduction form prepares a message on your device. It does not submit or save your details to a website database. You choose whether to send that message through your email app or LinkedIn. Those services process information under their own privacy policies.

If you contact Melusi, the information you choose to send will be used to respond and discuss your enquiry. You can request removal from follow-up contact through the same channel.

Hosting and external links

The site uses no advertising trackers or analytics cookies. Fonts are hosted with the site. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as IP address and browser information, to deliver and protect the site. Links to LinkedIn leave this website.

A future customer pilot

Access, storage, providers and retention for any confidential pilot documents will be agreed separately before documents are supplied. Do not send confidential technical files through this demonstration.
