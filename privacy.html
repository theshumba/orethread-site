Privacy, plainly.

This is the website of Orethread, an early-stage company founded by Melusi Ndoro. This notice was updated on 5 October 2026.

The interactive demo

The project, documents and figures in the demo are fictional. The demo does not accept documents or send anything to an AI provider. Your demo actions stay in the page and reset when you reload.

Getting in touch

The contact form prepares a note on your device, copies it and opens LinkedIn. It does not submit or store your details anywhere. You decide whether to send the note through LinkedIn, which processes it under its own privacy policy.

If you contact Melusi, what you choose to send is used to reply and to discuss your enquiry. You can ask to stop receiving follow-ups through the same channel.

Hosting

The site uses no advertising trackers and no analytics cookies. Fonts and images are hosted with the site. The hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process ordinary request information such as IP address and browser type to deliver and protect the site.

A future pilot

Storage location, access, providers and retention for any confidential pilot documents are agreed in writing before any document is shared. Please do not send confidential technical files through LinkedIn or this website.
